If your team already lives in Notion, Notion AI is a convenient way to get baseline AI features without leaving your workspace.

## Summarization and Q&A

The standout feature is **workspace Q&A** — ask a question and get an answer pulled from across your docs, with citations back to the source pages. This alone can justify the add-on for research-heavy teams.

## Writing assistance

Drafting and editing tools are solid for quick tasks (fixing grammar, adjusting tone, shortening text) but noticeably behind dedicated chat-based AI tools for long-form creative writing.

## Pricing

At **$8–10 per member/month** on top of your existing Notion plan, it's a meaningful add-on cost for larger teams, though the built-in Q&A can reduce time spent searching for information.

## Verdict

Best suited for existing Notion power users who want quick AI utility without adopting a separate tool — not a replacement for a dedicated AI writer.
