## What Is ProductsPilot?

ProductsPilot is a cloud-based software built around **four AI agents**:

* **Niche Finder** — researches products and niches based on demand and competition.
* **Product Engine** — creates complete digital product sets.
* **Listing Agent** — prepares listings, mockups, descriptions, tags, and pricing information.
* **Traffic Builder** — creates promotional assets across multiple organic traffic channels.

The goal is straightforward: help users move from **niche idea → digital product → listing → traffic** without needing to combine several different tools.

## How Does ProductsPilot Work?

The workflow is designed to be simple.

### 1. Choose a Niche

The Niche Finder shows opportunities with demand and competition information, helping users decide what type of digital product to create.

You can choose one of the suggested opportunities or enter your own niche.

### 2. Choose a Style

ProductsPilot provides different style directions that combine elements such as layout, palette, and typography.

Choose a style and start the product generation process.

### 3. Create the Product

The Product Engine creates a **complete matching product set**, rather than simply generating one image.

The front-end version supports up to **15 products per month** across eight product categories.

These include:

* Wall art
* Planners
* Digital planners
* Templates
* Invitations
* Kids and education
* Reports and guides
* Craft files

Products are prepared in print-ready formats with different sizes, mockups, and sizing information.

### 4. Prepare the Listing

The Listing Agent helps prepare the sales side of the product.

It can generate:

* Product titles
* Tags
* Descriptions
* Categories
* Lifestyle mockups
* Price ranges
* Trademark screening

This means you don't have to start your marketplace listing from a blank page.

### 5. Turn on Traffic

ProductsPilot also includes a **Traffic Builder** designed to create promotional content for seven traffic channels.

The system can use product mockups to create promotional assets such as pins, web stories, videos, and free guides.

The important distinction is that this is a traffic-generation system—not a guarantee that every product will generate sales.

## ProductsPilot and Etsy
