If you want to start selling chatbot setup as a service but don't want to build your entire agency model from scratch, the **AI Chatbot Agency Starter Kit** is designed to give you the business framework you need to get started.

Instead of teaching you how to code a chatbot, the product focuses on the **agency side of the business**: choosing services, pricing packages, finding clients, onboarding them, delivering projects, and creating recurring monthly revenue.

The system is delivered as a **Notion-based workspace** and is designed to work with chatbot platforms such as **Manychat, Voiceflow, Chatfuel, and Tidio**.

At the time of this review, the advertised price is **$97 one time**, compared with a stated regular price of $197.

### About This Review

This review is based on the information and product details presented on the official AI Chatbot Agency Starter Kit landing page. We've reviewed the advertised features, service packages, pricing, included resources, and overall positioning to help you understand what the kit offers before deciding whether it's right for you.

The review is based on the official product information and does not represent personal hands-on testing of the product.

## What Is the AI Chatbot Agency Starter Kit?

The AI Chatbot Agency Starter Kit is a collection of templates, scripts, workflows, pricing structures, and client-management resources intended for people who want to offer chatbot implementation as a service.

The main idea is straightforward:

**You sell the chatbot service. The kit gives you the agency infrastructure around that service.**

Rather than starting with a blank Notion page and trying to figure out what to offer clients, the kit provides seven predefined service packages, conversation scripts, a 30-day launch roadmap, onboarding resources, proposals, reporting tools, and business trackers.

It is therefore more of a **chatbot agency business starter system** than a chatbot-building software product.

## What Does It Include?

According to the official product page, the kit includes several components designed to cover different stages of running a chatbot service business.

### 1. Seven Pre-Priced Service Packages

One of the main components is a collection of seven chatbot service packages.

The packages include both a suggested setup fee and a monthly recurring fee.

| Service Package            | Setup Fee | Monthly Retainer |
| --------------------------- | --------: | ----------------: |
| Website FAQ / Lead Capture |      $497 |            $97/mo |
| Booking & Scheduling       |      $697 |           $147/mo |
| E-commerce Support         |      $797 |           $147/mo |
| Real Estate Inquiry        |      $797 |           $147/mo |
| Restaurant & Hospitality   |      $597 |            $97/mo |
| Review & Reputation        |      $697 |           $127/mo |
| Full-Service Retainer      |    $1,497 |           $297/mo |

This is potentially useful for beginners because pricing chatbot services can be difficult when you don't yet have a clear understanding of how to package the work.

The kit provides a starting point rather than requiring you to create your own pricing structure from zero.

### Website FAQ / Lead Capture

This package is positioned around answering common website questions while capturing visitor contact information. The listed price is **$497 setup + $97/month**.

### Booking & Scheduling

This package focuses on appointment booking through chat, with the sales page describing calendar synchronization as part of the service. The listed price is **$697 setup + $147/month**.

### E-commerce Support

This package is designed around customer-support use cases such as order status, returns, and product questions. The listed price is **$797 setup + $147/month**.

### Real Estate Inquiry

The real-estate package focuses on qualifying potential buyers or renters and routing qualified leads to an agent. The listed price is **$797 setup + $147/month**.

### Restaurant & Hospitality

This package targets restaurants and hospitality businesses, with use cases including opening hours, menus, reservations, and questions about gluten-free or allergy options. The listed price is **$597 setup + $97/month**.

### Review & Reputation

This package focuses on post-service follow-ups intended to encourage reviews and identify complaints before they become public. The listed price is **$697 setup + $127/month**.

### Full-Service Retainer

The highest-priced package combines the other services with additional integrations such as Messenger and WhatsApp, along with monthly conversation reporting. The listed price is **$1,497 setup + $297/month**.

### 2. Fifteen Conversation Flow Scripts

The product also includes **15 ready-to-use conversation flow scripts** covering six categories:

* FAQ
* Lead capture
* Booking
* Order status
* Reviews
* Escalation

These scripts are intended to provide a starting point when creating chatbot conversations for clients.

This can be particularly useful for someone who understands the technical side of chatbot platforms but doesn't want to write every conversation flow from scratch.

However, these should be treated as templates rather than universal scripts. A restaurant, dentist, real-estate agency, and online store will have very different customer journeys.

### 3. 14-Industry Use-Case Library

The kit also includes a use-case library covering **14 industries**.

The purpose is to help agency owners identify potential chatbot applications across different business categories.

This can make the prospecting process easier because instead of pitching a generic "AI chatbot," you can approach a business with a more specific problem and service package.

For example, a restaurant could be approached with a chatbot focused on reservations and menu questions, while a real-estate business could be approached with lead qualification.

### 4. 30-Day Agency Launch Roadmap

Another major component is the 30-day roadmap.

The official page divides the process into four stages.

#### Days 1–7: Pick Your Niche

The first stage focuses on choosing one or two packages and creating a demo chatbot.

#### Days 8–15: Build Your Pitch

The second stage focuses on preparing the sales process, including the discovery call script and proposal template.

#### Days 16–23: Land Client #1

The next stage focuses on onboarding and signing the first client using the provided onboarding flow and service agreement.

#### Days 24–30: Deliver & Expand

The final stage focuses on launching the chatbot, providing reporting, and potentially introducing an additional service package.

The advantage here is that the roadmap gives beginners a sequence to follow instead of simply handing them a large collection of templates.

### 5. Client Management Tools

The product isn't limited to sales scripts.

The official page also lists several resources for managing clients after the sale, including:

* Client onboarding system
* Service agreement template
* Monthly reporting system
* Discovery call script
* Proposal template
* Live Client Roster
* Revenue tracker
* Expense tracker

The client roster, revenue, and expense trackers are provided as **Notion databases**.

This is an important distinction: the product is designed around building an agency operation, not simply creating chatbot conversations.

## Which Chatbot Platforms Does It Support?

The sales page specifically mentions:

* Manychat
* Voiceflow
* Chatfuel
* Tidio

The kit therefore isn't presented as a standalone chatbot platform.

Instead, it provides the business framework around services that can be delivered using these existing chatbot tools.

That means you'll still need to understand the platform you choose and handle the actual implementation for your clients.

## Who Is This Product For?

The AI Chatbot Agency Starter Kit appears most relevant to people who want to **sell chatbot implementation as a service**.

It could be a good fit for:

### Freelancers
Freelancers who already provide web, marketing, automation, or digital services may be able to add chatbot setup as another service.

### Marketing Agencies
Agencies can potentially use the packages as an additional service for existing clients.

### Automation Specialists
People already working with no-code automation tools may find the business templates useful for turning their technical skills into a defined service offering.

### Beginners Exploring Chatbot Agencies
Someone who understands the basics of chatbot platforms but has no idea how to structure an agency offer may benefit from having packages, scripts, proposals, and onboarding resources already organized.

## Who Is It Probably Not For?

The product may not be the best fit for someone looking for a sophisticated AI chatbot platform itself.

It does **not appear to be a chatbot SaaS application** where you simply log in and build bots.

Instead, the product provides templates and business resources that you use alongside platforms such as Manychat, Voiceflow, Chatfuel, or Tidio.

You should therefore expect to do your own implementation and client work.

It may also be less useful for an experienced chatbot agency that already has its own:

* Pricing system
* Proposal templates
* Client onboarding process
* Conversation scripts
* Reporting system
* CRM or project management workflow

In that situation, much of the material may overlap with systems the agency already has.

## What Makes the Offer Interesting?

The most interesting part of the offer is the focus on **productized services**.

Instead of telling potential clients:

> "I can build you an AI chatbot."

You can structure your offer around a specific business problem.

For example:

**Restaurant chatbot → $597 setup + $97/month**

or

**Real-estate inquiry chatbot → $797 setup + $147/month**

This makes the service easier to understand and potentially easier to sell.

The recurring monthly component is also worth noting.

The model isn't presented purely as a one-time chatbot installation. Several packages include a monthly retainer, creating the possibility of recurring agency revenue.

Of course, the actual ability to generate clients and retain them will depend on your own marketing, sales, implementation quality, niche, pricing, and client results.

## Is $97 a Good Price?

At the advertised **$97 one-time price**, the value proposition depends largely on how much of the included material you would otherwise have to create yourself.

The kit includes:

* 7 service packages
* 15 conversation scripts
* 14-industry use cases
* 30-day launch roadmap
* Client onboarding resources
* Service agreement
* Discovery call script
* Proposal template
* Monthly reporting system
* Notion client and financial trackers

For someone starting from scratch, having these components organized in one system could save time.

For an experienced agency owner with established processes, the value may be considerably lower.

The important thing is to view the $97 as the price for the **business framework and templates**, not as a guaranteed shortcut to clients or revenue.

## Does It Guarantee Clients or Income?

No.

The product's own disclaimer explicitly states that it provides **no guarantee of income or success** and that results achieved by the product owner or other individuals aren't indicative of future results.

That's important because the package pricing shown on the sales page should be viewed as **suggested service pricing**, not guaranteed revenue.

For example, seeing a package priced at $697 + $147/month doesn't mean a new agency will automatically find customers willing to pay those prices.

Your results will depend on factors such as your niche, sales process, offer positioning, technical ability, market, and ability to deliver value.

## AI Chatbot Agency Starter Kit: Final Verdict

The **AI Chatbot Agency Starter Kit** is best understood as a **business-in-a-box framework for starting a chatbot service agency**, rather than an AI chatbot builder.

Its strongest feature is the combination of **productized service packages + pricing + sales resources + client management systems**.

For someone starting from zero, having seven defined offers, conversation scripts, a 30-day roadmap, proposal resources, onboarding templates, and Notion tracking systems could remove a lot of the initial setup work.

The biggest limitation is also straightforward: **the kit doesn't build the agency for you**.

You'll still need to choose a niche, learn the chatbot platform you're using, find prospects, sell the service, implement the chatbot, communicate with clients, and deliver ongoing value.

At the advertised **$97 one-time price**, it may make sense for a beginner who wants a structured starting point instead of building an agency system from scratch.

For an established chatbot or automation agency, however, the decision comes down to whether the included templates and workflows offer enough value beyond the systems you already have.

### Bottom line

**Best for:** Beginners, freelancers, and agencies looking to package chatbot setup as a service.

**Not ideal for:** People looking for a standalone chatbot platform or experienced agencies with established processes.

**Pricing:** $97 one time at the advertised offer price.

**Overall:** A practical starter framework for turning chatbot implementation into a structured service business, with the important caveat that the actual business results depend on execution and client acquisition.
