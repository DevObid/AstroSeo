Midjourney has consistently pushed the boundaries of what AI-generated images can look like, and version 6+ keeps that lead firmly intact.

## Image quality

Midjourney's biggest strength is aesthetic quality out of the box — lighting, composition, and texture look "art directed" even with simple prompts. It consistently outperforms competitors on subjective visual appeal.

## Workflow

The new **web app** removes the old dependency on Discord, adding a proper gallery, organized prompt history, and easier remixing. Discord is still supported for community-driven experimentation.

## Pricing

Plans start at **$10/month** for limited fast-generation hours, scaling up to unlimited relaxed generations on higher tiers. There's no longer a free trial, which is the main barrier to entry.

## Verdict

For illustrators, concept artists, and marketers who need striking visuals fast, Midjourney is still the benchmark other tools are measured against.
